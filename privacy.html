Çayxana Oyunları — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what Çayxana Oyunları — Game Table ("the game") does with information. The game is made by Çayxana Oyunları. Contact: Chayxana.oyunlari@gmail.com.

1. No accounts, no sign-up

You do not need to create an account, and the game does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.

2. What is stored on your device

Your scores, badges, settings (language, colours, music choice), game progress and gem balance are saved only on your own device, in the browser's or app's local storage. We do not receive them. Clearing your browser or app data erases them.

If you choose "Play my own music…", the audio file you pick is played on your device only and is not uploaded anywhere.

3. Advertising

The game is free. It may show banner ads, ads between games, and optional "watch an ad" offers that earn gems. All ads come from sponsors we deal with directly and choose ourselves. We do not use any advertising network.

  • The game does not use an advertising ID, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not show personalised ads.
  • We do not send your name, location, device identifiers or play history to sponsors.
  • A sponsor ad may show a picture or logo that is loaded from the sponsor's own website. Like any website picture, this lets that website see your IP address. If you tap an ad, it opens the sponsor's website, which has its own privacy policy.
  • If no sponsor is shown, the game shows only a plain placeholder box that collects nothing.

4. Gems and invite codes

Gems are free in-game points earned by watching ads or inviting friends. They have no cash value and cannot be bought or sold. Invite codes are random codes; using one does not reveal your identity.

5. Online-player counter (optional)

If switched on, the game contacts a small counting service to show how many people are playing. It sends a random anonymous identifier made on your device and the invite code, if any. It does not send your name, contacts or scores. If it is off, the game estimates busy times from the clock on your device.

5b. Offline copy

The website saves a copy of the game in your browser (a "service worker" cache) so it can work without internet. It contains only the game files, nothing about you. Clearing site data removes it.

5c. Clans, avatars and chat (optional)

If you join a clan, the game sends the clan service your random anonymous identifier, the avatar and display name you chose, and the short messages, emoji and stickers you send. Names are checked and rude ones are refused. Nothing else about you is sent. Leaving the clan removes your name and avatar from it.

If GIF search is switched on, the words you search for and your device's IP address are sent to GIPHY (giphy.com), which shows only G-rated GIFs and acts under its own privacy policy. A sent GIF is stored as a GIPHY reference number only.

6. Fonts, libraries and hosting

The offline version loads nothing from other websites for its fonts and graphics. The website version is hosted by Cloudflare, which may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP address) for security.

7. Children

The game is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are chosen by us, come only from direct sponsors, and are not personalised or based on tracking. We do not knowingly accept ads that are unsuitable for a general family audience.

7b. Security and Google Play Protect

We confirm that Çayxana Oyunları is a simple games app and is not harmful software. The app asks only for internet access (for the optional online features). It does not ask for access to your SMS, contacts, call history, microphone, camera, location, accessibility services or other apps. In Google Play Protect's terms, the app contains none of the following:

  • Backdoor — no hidden code that lets anyone control your device remotely.
  • Billing fraud — no SMS, calls or hidden charges. Gems are free points and nothing in the app is bought with real money.
  • Commercial spyware and spyware — the app does not monitor your activity or location and does not read your messages, photos, audio or call history.
  • Denial of service (DoS) — the app does not send floods of requests to any server or attack any system.
  • Hostile downloader — the app does not install other apps and does not download or run code from outside.
  • Non-Android threat — the app contains nothing aimed at Windows, Mac or other devices.
  • Phishing — the app never asks for passwords, bank details or identity information, and it does not pretend to be another app or company.
  • Elevated privilege abuse and rooting — the app does not try to bypass Android's protections or root your device.
  • Ransomware — the app does not lock your device or data, or demand money.
  • Spam — the app does not send messages to your contacts or anyone else without your action.

The app can be removed like any other app, from your device's normal app settings. It is built for current versions of Android and is signed with our own developer key. If you find a security problem, please write to Chayxana.oyunlari@gmail.com.

8. Your choices

You can delete everything the game has saved by clearing its data in your browser or app settings. For any question or request, write to Chayxana.oyunlari@gmail.com.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
